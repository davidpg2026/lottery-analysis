About this site. David's Lottery Analysis is a free educational and statistical resource. It analyzes publicly available historical lottery results to illustrate concepts like frequency, probability, distribution, and randomness. It is not a gambling service, does not sell tickets, and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any state lottery, Powerball, Mega Millions, or any lottery operator.

No predictions, no edge. Lottery drawings are independent random events. Every combination has the same probability every draw. Nothing on this site — including the "hot/cold," "overdue," "consensus," or quick-pick features — can predict results or improve anyone's odds of winning. All content is provided for curiosity and entertainment only. Results shown here are unofficial; always verify winning numbers and claim prizes through the official lottery for your state.

Play responsibly · 18+. You must be 18 or older (19+ in some states) to play the lottery. If gambling stops being fun or feels like a problem, help is available and confidential: call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537). Please play within your means.

Data sources: New York State Open Data (data.ny.gov) for Powerball, Mega Millions & Millionaire For Life (multi-state games with identical numbers nationwide) and the New York games (NY Lotto, Take 5, Numbers, Win 4, Pick 10, Quick Draw); official illinoislottery.com results for the Illinois games (IL Lotto, Lucky Day Lotto, Pick 3 & Pick 4), updated manually. All analysis runs in your browser; no personal data is collected.
